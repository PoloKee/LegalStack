# LegalStack Architect — Elevator Pitch

Indie founders and solo developers are usually their own legal department, bookkeeper, and compliance officer — and things fall through the cracks. LegalStack Architect is a suite built for exactly that person. It brings legal work, financial separation, and compliance into one workspace, and adds portfolio triage for builders running many apps at once — the design is sized around a 42-app portfolio. Instead of scattered folders and guesswork, a solo founder gets one place that keeps the business side of building organized, separated, and reviewable.

**Tagline:** A founder's legal and compliance command center.
